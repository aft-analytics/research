# County recorder DIY

Public recorder/assessor portals for deed index + images — **jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction**, not a US API.

**Pattern:** Government landing page often deep-links to a **private** search vendor (see [DC OTR ROD images](https://otr.cfo.dc.gov/service/recorder-deeds-document-images)). Structured free dumps are rare; best open example so far is NYC [ACRIS](nyc-open-data.md).

**Useful for us?** Spot verification / samples. For national ownership overlays → [property-data-vendors.md](property-data-vendors.md) / [deeds-by-state.md](deeds-by-state.md).
