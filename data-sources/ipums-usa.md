# IPUMS USA (census microdata)

[usa.ipums.org/usa](https://usa.ipums.org/usa/) — Minnesota Population Center. Free with registration.

**What it is:** Harmonized **person/household microdata** from U.S. decennial censuses (samples + some full-count historical) and **ACS** (2000–present, incl. recent 1-year). Custom extracts + online analysis. Related: [IPUMS CPS](https://cps.ipums.org/), [NHGIS](https://www.nhgis.org/) (aggregates/GIS).

**Grain:** individual / household survey records — **not** address, parcel, or firm. Public ACS via IPUMS is **PUMS** geography (PUMA ~100k people), not block/parcel.

**Useful for:** richer income/housing/employment distributions than published ACS tables; research on borrower-like populations; spectrum / mix studies.  
**Not for:** KYB, deeds, SOS, permit overlays, or joining to a loan book by address.

Vs [Census hub](census.md): Census publishes PUMS too; IPUMS adds harmonization, docs, and extract tooling. Prefer IPUMS when building custom person-level research samples; prefer data.census.gov tables/API for standard geo aggregates.
