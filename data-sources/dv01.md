End-to-end loan data management, reporting, and analytics. Public offerings include:

| Area | What it is |
|------|------------|
| **Tape Cracker** | Ingest / normalize / validate dirty loan tapes (ML field mapping, strats, custom calcs, exports) |
| **Deal / portfolio reporting** | Loan-level performance dashboards for securitizations, whole-loan portfolios, credit facilities |
| **Cashflow & analytics** | Loan- and deal-level cashflows, CPR/CDR/CNL, cohorting, stress scenarios |

## How their model works (originators ↔ investors)

Originators in their network (e.g. SoFi and peers) provide **loan tapes** / loan-level feeds. dv01 cleanses, standardizes, and publishes polished **dashboards and analytics** for those lenders’ **investors and funding** stakeholders — including Loan Data Agent–style roles on securitizations (direct-from-issuer data into investor reporting). That exchange is the core reciprocal value; we are primarily a **consumer** of tape-style data and analytics, not an issuer feeding their platform.

Public SoFi partnerships (consumer + student loan securitizations) illustrate the pattern: issuer supplies loan-level data → dv01 normalizes → investors monitor pools on the platform. Their customer / lender set is itself a useful signal of which originators sit in that capital-markets reporting network.
