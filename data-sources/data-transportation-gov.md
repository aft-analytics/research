# U.S. DOT Open Data

[data.transportation.gov](https://data.transportation.gov/) — Socrata ODP. Federates heavily with [data.bts.gov](https://data.bts.gov/) (Bureau of Transportation Statistics).

## Worth noting for us

| Asset | Why |
|-------|-----|
| **FMCSA Company Census File** ([az4n-8mr2](https://data.transportation.gov/d/az4n-8mr2)) | USDOT-numbered carriers (active/inactive/pending); phones, officers, ops — trucking/fleet entity list, not SOS LLCs |
| NHTSA recalls / investigations by manufacturer | Auto safety (thin for consumer credit) |
| **BTS** catalog | Freight, mobility, border crossings, transport economic indicators — market/geo context |

## Not useful for core ask

No deeds, SOS entities, building permits, or borrower microdata. Most of the portal is transport ops/safety/stats.

**Takeaway:** Grab FMCSA census if we care about commercial carriers; use BTS for freight/mobility overlays. Otherwise skip vs Census / SOS / city portals.
