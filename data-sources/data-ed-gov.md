# U.S. Department of Education Open Data

[data.ed.gov](https://data.ed.gov/) — ED CKAN catalog (~1.1k data profiles). Topics include K-12, special ed, postsecondary, **student aid**.

## Relevant to credit / risk (student-loan adjacent)

| Dataset area | Notes |
|--------------|--------|
| [College Scorecard](https://data.ed.gov/dataset?q=college+scorecard) | School-level outcomes, debt, earnings |
| Federal Student Loan Portfolio / OPE loan volume | Aggregate FSA portfolio stats |
| Cohort / school **default rates** | School-level CDR — not borrower microdata |
| Title IV volume reports | Aid volume by program/school |
| NPSAS / IPEDS | Survey & institution stats (NCES) |
| NSLDS (catalog entry) | System of record for aid — **public extracts are limited**; not a free full borrower dump |

**Useful for:** education-debt market research, school risk overlays, Scorecard joins.  
**Not for:** general consumer lending book features, SOS/housing/permits, or unrestricted borrower-level NSLDS.

Prefer primary FSA/NCES download pages linked from each profile when building pipelines.
