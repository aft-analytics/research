# Purdue GIS guide — U.S. data by state

[guides.lib.purdue.edu — U.S. Data by State](https://guides.lib.purdue.edu/c.php?g=353290&p=2378621) (Purdue Libraries GIS Data Guide). Part of [GIS Data Guide](https://guides.lib.purdue.edu/gis).

**What it is:** Curated links to **state GIS / geospatial clearinghouses** (parcels, boundaries, imagery, env/transport layers) — not SOS entity catalogs or Statista-style stats. ~137 outbound links extracted from the page; many are old (BLM deep links, university servers) and need re-checking.

**Overlap with our ask:** Best for **parcels / land / local building geography** once you have an address or county. Complements [Forbes state open-data list](state-open-data-portals.md) (tabular portals) and [NYC Open Data](nyc-open-data.md).

## Mostly skip for credit/KYB

Geology surveys, forest/BLM layers, shoreline/imagery, university course GIS pages — useful for mapping, not firm ownership or borrower risk features.

**Limit:** Guide ≠ verified live inventory. Prefer current state GIS “hub/clearinghouse” home pages; use for parcel/address enrichment after entity/deed sources.
