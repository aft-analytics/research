# SATT ANALYTICS — US Building Permits (Residential)

[Databricks Marketplace listing](https://marketplace.databricks.com/details/d340823d-485b-4494-98cb-0c299f7a1de2/SATT-ANALYTICS_US-Building-Permits-Data-Residential) · [provider](https://marketplace.databricks.com/provider/cd22ef23-30ee-437c-aef3-fd13550a2087/SATT-ANALYTICS) · [sattanalytics.com](https://www.sattanalytics.com/) · [pricing](https://www.sattanalytics.com/building-permits-data-pricing)

**What it is:** Paid Databricks share of cleaned residential permit/project records (new build, addition, remodel, renovation) for major US (+ Canada) jurisdictions. Listing scale: ~300K active projects, ~150K properties, ~80K contractors; **weekly** refresh of a **rolling 6-week** window; daily collection claimed. Fields: address, permit type/status, description, estimated value, category, timestamps; contractor/applicant/developer/contacts when available. History (1–20y) on request. Sibling commercial-permits / property SKUs on Marketplace.

**Pricing:** Marketplace = **Paid / by request** (no public $). Vendor site: Essential **$199/mo** (state, 3.5k MRR exports); Professional **$1,999/mo** (nationwide, 30k); Enterprise custom (Databricks/Snowflake/AWS + history); overage **$0.10/record**.

**vs peers:** Not Census **BPS** / [HUD SOCDS](hud-socds-permits.md) (free place/county *aggregates* of new residential units). Same commercial microdata class as [BuildZoom](buildzoom.md) / Shovels — address/project grain for renovation + contacts; buy only after sample vs those, not instead of free BPS for market totals.
